## Description (4000 max)
IMSTUDY is a flashcard app built for Apple Pencil. Write and draw your cards by hand, add photos
and typed text, then study them with spaced repetition so you remember more in less time.

WRITE LIKE ON PAPER
• Handwrite and draw on every card with Apple Pencil, with the full pen, highlighter and eraser palette
• Pinch to zoom in and write small. Lines and ink scale together
• Lined or blank paper, set separately for the front and the back
• Light, dark or automatic appearance

MAKE CARDS YOUR WAY
• Two-sided cards that flip with a tap
• Add typed text boxes anywhere: tap to edit, drag to move, scale to fit
• Add photos, move and resize them, fade them with the opacity slider to trace over them, and
  arrange them front-to-back. Your writing always stays on top
• Move or scale all your handwriting and highlights as one piece
• Star the cards that matter most

STUDY SMARTER
• Spaced repetition: mark each card Know It or Don't Know, and cards return right when you need them
• Study what's due, everything, or only your starred cards
• Daily streak, accuracy and study-time stats, with a 7-day chart

FIND ANYTHING
• Search across all your decks, including your handwriting

STAY IN CONTROL
• Undo for deletes, plus a Recently Deleted folder that keeps cards and decks for 30 days
• Duplicate cards, or move and copy them between decks
• Back up your decks to a file and restore them any time

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• No account, no ads, no tracking. Everything stays on your iPad
• Handwriting search runs on your device

Made for iPad. Best with Apple Pencil.
