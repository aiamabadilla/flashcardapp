# IMSTUDY Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** October 14, 2026

IMSTUDY ("the app") is a flashcard app for iPad. This policy explains what happens to your
information when you use it. The short version: **everything stays on your device, and we collect
nothing.**

## What we collect

**Nothing.** IMSTUDY does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal information. There are
no accounts, no sign-in, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking.

## What the app stores on your device

To work, the app keeps the following **only on your iPad**:

- Your decks and flashcards: your handwriting and drawings, typed text, and photos you add.
- Your study progress: which cards you know, when they are due, and your study streak and statistics.
- Your settings, such as light or dark appearance.

This information is never sent to us or to anyone else. If you delete the app, it is removed from
your device. Backups you make with the app's Back Up feature are files that you choose where to save
or send; we never receive them.

## Photos

If you add a photo to a card, the app uses the system photo picker, which only gives the app the
specific photos you select. A copy of each selected photo is stored inside the app on your device.

## Handwriting search

The app reads the text in your handwriting so that you can search your cards. This happens entirely
on your device using Apple's on-device text recognition. Nothing is uploaded.

## Children

The app does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

## Third parties

The app contains no third-party code that collects data, and it makes no network requests.
